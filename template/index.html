FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What Types Of Coffee Do You Offer?

We offer single-origin coffees, signature blends, espresso roasts, and seasonal selections.

2. How Do I Choose The Right Coffee For Me?

Choose by the flavor notes and brewing method you enjoy, or ask our team for a recommendation.

3. Do You Offer Coffee Subscriptions?

Yes. Our subscriptions deliver freshly roasted coffee on a recurring schedule.

4. What Grind Sizes Do You Offer?

We grind for espresso, moka pot, pour-over, filter, AeroPress, and French press.

5. How Should I Store My Coffee?

Keep coffee sealed in a cool, dry place away from heat, light, moisture, and strong odors.

6. Where Is Your Coffee Sourced From?

Our coffees are sourced from trusted producers across renowned coffee-growing regions.

7. Do You Have Organic Or Fair-Trade Options?

Yes. Availability changes seasonally; ask our team about the current certified selections.

8. How Can I Make The Best Coffee At Home?

Use freshly roasted coffee, filtered water, a consistent ratio, and the right grind for your brewer.
